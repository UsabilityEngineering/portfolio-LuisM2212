# Amazon Reordering Experience


I used the Amazon mobile app to reorder fish oil pills that I regularly buy. My main goal was to complete the purchase as quickly as possible without having to think too much about brand comparisons or product details. Since this was a repeat purchase, I already knew exactly what I wanted and expected the app to help me get to checkout with minimal effort.
I opened the Amazon app and tapped on the search bar, then typed “fish oil pills.” Almost immediately, the brand I usually purchase appeared at the top of the search results. This reduced my need to scroll or compare options, which kept the interaction focused and efficient. I tapped on the product, confirmed it was the correct item, and selected the Buy Now button to complete the order.
Throughout this process, the app provided strong **feedback**, meaning it clearly communicated that my actions were being recognized and processed. For example, the search results updated instantly as I typed, and the Buy Now button responded immediately when tapped. These system responses reassured me that the app was functioning correctly and that I was progressing toward my goal without any errors or delays.

The experience also involved very low **cognitive load**, which refers to the amount of mental effort required to complete a task. Because Amazon remembered my previous purchases and surfaced the correct product right away, I didn’t have to spend time evaluating alternatives or recalling specific product details. The interface handled most of the decision-making for me, allowing me to complete the task quickly and with little conscious effort.
